PhD programme

Giovanni Paolo Romano coordinates the PhD programme in Theoretical and Applied Mechanics, hosted by the Department of Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering. The programme covers fluid dynamics, applied mechanics, and mechanics of solids and structures; our PhD students carry out their research within it.
