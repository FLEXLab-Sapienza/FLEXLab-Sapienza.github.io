PhD programmes

Group members serve on the boards of two doctoral programmes hosted by the Department of Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering.

Theoretical and Applied Mechanics

Coordinated by Giovanni Paolo Romano. The programme covers fluid dynamics, applied mechanics, and mechanics of solids and structures.

Aeronautics and Space Engineering

Mario Tindaro Migliorino is a member of the PhD board. Research areas include fluid dynamics, aeroelasticity, propulsion, flight mechanics and space missions.
